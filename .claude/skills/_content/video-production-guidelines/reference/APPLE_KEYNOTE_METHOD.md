# Apple Keynote Methodology

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Domain:** Video
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-03

---

## Overview

This document applies Steve Jobs' presentation principles from Apple Keynote events to MYCURE video
production, focusing on one message per scene, visual-audio harmony, and progressive disclosure.

---

## Why Apple Keynote Principles?

### Steve Jobs' Mastery

**Apple Keynote presentations were legendary:**

- Clear, memorable messages
- Visuals that supported (not duplicated) speech
- Progressive reveal creating anticipation
- Simplified complex technology

**These principles translate perfectly to video:**

- Viewer attention span is short (like live audience)
- Visual medium requires visual thinking
- One idea at a time prevents overwhelm
- Progressive disclosure maintains engagement

---

## The Four Core Principles

### Principle 1: One Message Per Scene

**Each scene communicates single idea.**

#### Why This Matters

**Human brain processes information sequentially:**

- Multiple messages simultaneously = confusion
- Single focused message = clarity and retention
- Viewer can only remember 1-2 ideas from each scene

#### Application to Video

**❌ Don't:** Pack multiple features into one scene

```markdown
Scene 3: MYCURE Features

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 3: Dashboard with all modules] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE has auto-fill, real-time validation, cross-module sync, FHSIS reporting, inventory tracking, and appointment reminders. |

Problem: 6 features in 1 scene - viewer overwhelmed, remembers none
```

**✅ Do:** One feature per scene

```markdown
Scene 3: Auto-Fill Feature

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 3: Registration form auto-filling] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE's auto-fill feature saves 70% of data entry time. |

Scene 4: Real-Time Validation

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 4: Validation checking fields] | [VOICEOVER] Real-time validation catches errors before they become problems. |

Scene 5: Cross-Module Sync

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 5: Data flowing between modules] | [VOICEOVER] Data syncs instantly across all modules. |

Result: 3 clear messages, each memorable
```

#### Testing Your Scene

**Ask: "What is the ONE thing I want viewers to remember from this scene?"**

If answer is:

- Single idea → Good scene
- Multiple ideas → Split into multiple scenes
- Unclear → Refocus or cut scene

### Principle 2: Visual Supports Audio (Doesn't Duplicate)

**Visuals and audio should complement, not repeat.**

#### Why This Matters

**Redundancy wastes cognitive bandwidth:**

- Reading text while hearing same text = processing same info twice
- Viewer's brain isn't learning more, just duplicating effort
- Wastes precious screen time

**Visual-audio harmony doubles impact:**

- Audio explains WHY (concept, benefit, reason)
- Visual shows HOW (demonstration, proof, example)
- Viewer processes two complementary streams = deeper understanding

#### Application to Video

**❌ Don't:** Show what you're saying

```markdown
| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [GRAPHIC: "MYCURE automates patient registration"] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE automates patient registration. |
| [GRAPHIC: "Saves 70% of time"] | [VOICEOVER] And saves 70% of time. |

Problem: Viewer reads text, hears same text - redundant, boring
```

**✅ Do:** Show the benefit while explaining it

```markdown
| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [B-ROLL: Patient form auto-filling in 3 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE automates patient registration. |
| [B-ROLL: Receptionist registering 10 patients in time it used to take for 3] | [VOICEOVER] That's 70% less time on data entry. |

Result: Visual PROVES claim, audio EXPLAINS benefit - complementary, impactful
```

#### Exceptions: When Text is OK

**Text overlays are appropriate for:**

1. **Statistics** - "73% of clinics", "10 hours saved per week"
2. **Key terms** - Technical words viewer should remember
3. **CTAs** - Contact info, links, demo booking
4. **Lower thirds** - Names, titles, locations

**Even then, keep text minimal:**

- 5-7 words maximum
- High contrast (readable)
- On screen 3-5 seconds minimum

### Principle 3: The 3-Second Rule

**Every visual element must be on screen at least 3 seconds.**

#### Why This Matters

**Human visual processing takes time:**

- 0-1 second: Brain registers something changed
- 1-2 seconds: Brain identifies what it's seeing
- 2-3 seconds: Brain understands meaning/context
- 3+ seconds: Brain integrates information

**Cutting faster than 3 seconds:**

- Viewer feels rushed, anxious
- Information doesn't sink in
- Looks amateur, confusing

#### Application to Video

**Timing guidelines:**

- **Text overlays:** 3-5 seconds minimum
- **Graphics:** 3-4 seconds minimum
- **B-roll shots:** 3-6 seconds per shot
- **Transitions:** 0.5-1 second (quick, don't linger)
- **Scene changes:** 10+ seconds minimum (Principle 1)

**❌ Don't:** Rapid cuts

```
[B-ROLL: Typing - 1 second]
[B-ROLL: Clicking - 1 second]
[B-ROLL: Scrolling - 1 second]
[B-ROLL: Saving - 1 second]

Problem: 4 shots in 4 seconds - viewer can't process, feels hectic
```

**✅ Do:** Sustained shots

```
[B-ROLL: Typing patient name, auto-fill appearing - 4 seconds]
[B-ROLL: Clicking suggestion, form filling - 3 seconds]
[B-ROLL: Reviewing complete form - 3 seconds]

Result: 3 shots in 10 seconds - viewer follows action, understands process
```

#### Exception: Fast Montages

**Fast cuts are OK for:**

- **Montages** - Conveying passage of time, variety
- **Energy sequences** - Creating excitement, momentum
- **Transitions** - Between major scenes

**But even then:**

- Individual clips: 1-2 seconds (slightly faster OK)
- Entire montage: 5-10 seconds (don't overuse)

---

### Principle 4: Progressive Disclosure

**Reveal information gradually, build complexity step-by-step.**

#### Why This Matters

**Learning is sequential:**

- Simple concepts first, complex later
- Build on previous understanding
- Prevent overwhelm

**Progressive disclosure creates anticipation:**

- Viewer wonders "what's next?"
- Maintains engagement throughout video
- Makes complex topics accessible

#### Application to Video

**Structure scenes from simple to complex:**

**Example: Registration feature**

```
Scene 1: Problem statement (simple)
- Manual registration is slow

Scene 2: Solution introduction (simple)
- MYCURE automates registration

Scene 3: First capability (medium)
- Auto-fill feature demo

Scene 4: Second capability (medium)
- Real-time validation demo

Scene 5: Third capability (medium-complex)
- Cross-module sync demo

Scene 6: Complete workflow (complex)
- All features working together

Scene 7: Results (simple)
- Time savings achieved
```

**Flow:** Simple → Build → Build → Build → Integrate → Simple

**❌ Don't:** Start with complexity

```markdown
Scene 1: Complete Workflow

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 1: All Registration features working simultaneously] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE's Registration module uses auto-fill with real-time validation and cross-module synchronization while generating FHSIS reports... |

Problem: Viewer lost immediately, no foundation
```

**✅ Do:** Build gradually

```markdown
Scene 1: The Basics

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 1: Empty patient form] | [VOICEOVER] Patient registration starts with a simple form. |

Scene 2: First Enhancement

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 2: Auto-fill suggestions appearing] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE adds auto-fill to save time. |

Scene 3: Second Enhancement

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 3: Validation checking fields] | [VOICEOVER] Real-time validation catches errors. |

Result: Viewer follows progression, understands each layer
```

## Applying All Four Principles Together

### Example: Registration Module Demo

**Outline:**

```
Scene 1: Establish problem (Principle 4 - start simple)
Scene 2: Introduce solution (Principle 1 - one message: MYCURE automates registration)
Scene 3: Auto-fill demo (Principle 2 - show feature working, explain benefit)
Scene 4: Validation demo (Principle 3 - sustained shots, 3+ seconds each)
Scene 5: Sync demo (Principle 4 - building complexity)
Scene 6: Complete workflow (Principle 1 - one message: all features integrated)
```

**Scene 3 detailed example:**

```markdown
## Scene 3: Auto-Fill Feature Demo

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 3: Registration form, empty "Name" field] | [VOICEOVER] Let's see auto-fill in action. |

[Principle 1: Scene focuses on ONE feature - auto-fill]

| [B-ROLL: Typing "Maria San" - 4 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] Start typing a patient's name... |

[Principle 3: 4 seconds on screen - viewer processes action]

| [B-ROLL: Auto-fill dropdown with 3 suggestions - 3 seconds] | [SFX: Notification chime] |

[Principle 2: Visual SHOWS feature, audio doesn't repeat "suggestions appear"]
[Principle 3: 3 seconds for viewer to read suggestions]

| [B-ROLL: Clicking "Maria Santos" - 3 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] ...and MYCURE finds matching records. |

[Principle 3: 3 seconds to show selection]

| [B-ROLL: Form auto-fills with data - 5 seconds] | [SFX: Success chime] |
| [GRAPHIC: "70% time savings" appears - 3 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] That's 70% less manual data entry. |

[Principle 2: Visual PROVES claim (fast auto-fill), audio QUANTIFIES benefit]
[Principle 3: 3 seconds for stat to register]

**Timing:** 18 seconds total
**Purpose:** Demonstrate auto-fill feature and quantify benefit
**Transition:** Cut to Scene 4 (Validation feature)
```

[Principle 4: Progressive - auto-fill is first (simplest) feature, sets foundation]

## Keynote-Style Scene Patterns

### Pattern 1: "Here's What" → "Here's How" → "Here's Why"

**Structure:**

1. **What:** Introduce feature
2. **How:** Demonstrate functionality
3. **Why:** Explain benefit

**Example:**

```markdown
Scene 5: Inventory Tracking

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [GRAPHIC: "Real-Time Inventory Tracking"] | [VOICEOVER] Real-time inventory tracking. |

[What: Feature name]

| [B-ROLL: Inventory dashboard showing stock levels across 3 locations] | [VOICEOVER] See stock levels at all your clinics instantly. |

[How: Demonstration]

| [GRAPHIC: "Never run out of critical medications"] | [VOICEOVER] So you never run out of critical medications. |

[Why: Benefit]
```

### Pattern 2: "Before" → "After"

**Structure:**

1. **Before:** Show problem state
2. **After:** Show solution state
3. **Contrast:** Emphasize difference

**Example:**

```markdown
Scene 2: Time Savings

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [B-ROLL: Receptionist manually entering data, clock showing 15 minutes elapsed] | [VOICEOVER] Manual registration: 15 minutes per patient. |

[Before: Problem state]

| [B-ROLL: MYCURE auto-fill completing registration, clock showing 5 minutes] | [VOICEOVER] With MYCURE: 5 minutes per patient. |

[After: Solution state]

| [GRAPHIC: "15 min → 5 min" comparison] | [VOICEOVER] That's 10 minutes saved per patient. |

[Contrast: Emphasize difference]
```

### Pattern 3: "One More Thing"

**Structure:**

1. **Expected features:** Cover standard capabilities
2. **Unexpected feature:** Introduce surprising bonus
3. **Impact:** Explain why it matters

**Example:**

```markdown
Scene 8: Standard Features Covered

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 8: Dashboard showing Registration, Appointment, Billing modules] | [VOICEOVER] Registration, appointments, billing. Everything you'd expect. |

[Expected: Standard features]

Scene 9: Surprise Feature

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 9: FHSIS report auto-generating] | [VOICEOVER] But here's the best part... |
| [GRAPHIC: "Automated FHSIS Reporting" appears] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE automatically generates DOH FHSIS reports. |

[Unexpected: Bonus feature]

| [GRAPHIC: "4-8 hours saved per week"] | [VOICEOVER] That's 4 to 8 hours saved every week on government reporting. |

[Impact: Why it matters]
```

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Violating One Message Rule

**❌ Wrong:**

```markdown
Scene 3: MYCURE Features

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Dashboard] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE automates registration, schedules appointments, tracks inventory, generates reports, and manages billing. |

Problem: 5 messages in one scene - viewer overwhelmed
```

**✅ Correct:**

```markdown
Scene 3: Registration Automation

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Registration demo] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE automates patient registration. |

Scene 4: Appointment Scheduling

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Appointment demo] | [VOICEOVER] Scheduling appointments is effortless. |

Result: 2 clear, memorable messages
```

### Mistake 2: Visual Duplicates Audio

**❌ Wrong:**

```markdown
| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [GRAPHIC: "MYCURE saves time"] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE saves time. |

Problem: Redundant - viewer reads and hears same thing
```

**✅ Correct:**

```markdown
| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [B-ROLL: Registration completing in 5 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE saves time. |

Result: Visual PROVES claim, audio STATES claim
```

### Mistake 3: Too Fast

**❌ Wrong:**

```
[Shot 1: 1 second]
[Shot 2: 1.5 seconds]
[Shot 3: 1 second]

Problem: Viewer can't process, feels rushed
```

**✅ Correct:**

```
[Shot 1: 4 seconds]
[Shot 2: 3 seconds]
[Shot 3: 5 seconds]

Result: Viewer follows action, understands context
```

### Mistake 4: Starting Too Complex

**❌ Wrong:**

```markdown
Scene 1: Complete System Architecture

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Complex diagram with 6 modules, database, APIs] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE's architecture integrates... |

Problem: Viewer has no foundation, lost immediately
```

**✅ Correct:**

```markdown
Scene 1: Simple Overview

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Dashboard with 6 module icons] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE has 6 modules working together. |

Scene 2: Deep Dive Begins

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [Registration module highlighted] | [VOICEOVER] Let's start with Registration. |

Result: Simple foundation, then build complexity
```

## Checklist: Keynote Principles

### Before Production

**Principle 1: One Message Per Scene**

- [ ] Each scene has single, clear focus
- [ ] If scene has multiple ideas, split into separate scenes
- [ ] Scene title reflects the ONE message

**Principle 2: Visual Supports Audio**

- [ ] Visual shows what audio explains (not duplicate)
- [ ] No on-screen text that narrator reads word-for-word
- [ ] Visual provides proof/example while audio provides concept/benefit

**Principle 3: 3-Second Rule**

- [ ] Text overlays on screen 3-5 seconds minimum
- [ ] B-roll shots sustained 3-6 seconds each
- [ ] No rapid cuts faster than 3 seconds (unless intentional montage)

**Principle 4: Progressive Disclosure**

- [ ] Scenes build from simple to complex
- [ ] Each scene builds on previous understanding
- [ ] Final scenes integrate concepts introduced earlier

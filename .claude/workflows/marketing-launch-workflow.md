# Marketing Launch Workflow

**Purpose:** Coordinated marketing campaign for MYCURE feature releases

**When to use:** Product launches, feature announcements, major updates

**Estimated time:** 5-7 days (planning through publication)

---

## Overview

Successful marketing requires coordinated content across channels: social media posts, demo videos, and landing pages. This workflow ensures consistent messaging and MYCURE brand standards across all touchpoints.

**Quick start:** Run `/create-social-post` for social content

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Campaign Planning (Day 1)

**Define Campaign Goals:**
- [ ] Awareness (reach, impressions)
- [ ] Lead generation (demo requests, email signups)
- [ ] Conversions (paid subscriptions, trials)

**Identify Key Message:**
```
One sentence that captures the feature value

Example: "MYCURE's multi-location stock transfer saves Philippine clinics 75% of transfer time."
```

**Select Content Pillar:**
- Educational (30%) - Tips, best practices
- Product Value (25%) - Features, benefits
- Industry Leadership (20%) - Trends, insights
- Community Building (15%) - Stories, testimonials
- Health Awareness (10%) - Public health content

**Target Audience:**
- Primary: Philippine clinic administrators
- Secondary: Healthcare professionals (doctors, pharmacists)
- Tertiary: Healthcare IT decision makers

**Output:** Campaign brief with goals, message, pillar, audience

### Step 3: Video Production (Days 3-5)

**Skill:** `video-production-guidelines`

**Script Using Apple Keynote Methodology:**

```markdown
# MYCURE Stock Transfer Demo Video

**Type:** Product Demo
**Duration:** 1:30
**Key Message:** Stock transfers are 75% faster with MYCURE

---

## Scene 1: Hook (10 seconds)

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 1: Clinic staff manually tracking stock in Excel, frustrated expression] | [VOICEOVER] Stock transfers shouldn't take 15 minutes. |
| [B-ROLL: Clock ticking, papers stacking up] | [VOICEOVER] There's a better way. |
| [GRAPHIC: "What if it only took 3 minutes?"] | [MUSIC: Upbeat transition] |

---

## Scene 2: Problem (15 seconds)

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 2: Split screen - Manual process vs MYCURE] | [VOICEOVER] Traditional stock tracking wastes time. |
| [B-ROLL: Staff searching through spreadsheets] | [VOICEOVER] Fifteen minutes per transfer. |
| [GRAPHIC: "15 minutes" fades to "3 minutes"] | [VOICEOVER] MYCURE cuts that to 3 minutes. |

---

## Scene 3: Demo (45 seconds)

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 3: MYCURE interface, Transfer Stock button] | [VOICEOVER] See how it works. |
| [B-ROLL: Click Transfer, select locations] | [VOICEOVER] Choose source and destination. |
| [B-ROLL: Enter item, quantity, lot number] | [VOICEOVER] Enter details. MYCURE validates in real-time. |
| [B-ROLL: Confirmation screen, 3 seconds] | [VOICEOVER] Done. Three minutes. Complete audit trail. |
| [GRAPHIC: "75% time savings" appears] | [VOICEOVER] That's 75 percent less time on admin. |

---

## Scene 4: Benefit (15 seconds)

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 4: Happy clinic staff, serving patients] | [VOICEOVER] Less paperwork means more time for patients. |
| [B-ROLL: Smiling patients, efficient workflow] | [VOICEOVER] Streamlined operations. Better care. |

---

## Scene 5: CTA (5 seconds)

| VISUAL | AUDIO |
|--------|-------|
| [SCENE 5: MYCURE logo, demo booking link] | [VOICEOVER] See MYCURE in action. |
| [GRAPHIC: "Book demo: mycure.md"] | [MUSIC: Upbeat conclusion] |
```

**Production Steps:**
1. Record screen demo of MYCURE stock transfer
2. Film B-roll of clinic operations (with consent)
3. Record professional voiceover (Filipino narrator preferred)
4. Edit using script timing (word count ÷ 2.5 = seconds)
5. Add captions for accessibility
6. Export 1920x1080, 30fps, MP4

**Publish:**
- YouTube (main channel)
- Embed on landing page
- Share on social media

**Output:** 1:30 demo video with captions

### Step 4: Landing Page (Days 4-5)

**Skill:** `frontend-design`
**Command:** `/design-ui`

**Design Requirements:**

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Space Grotesk (geometric, modern)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (professional, readable)
- Data/Stats: JetBrains Mono (for "75%" and metrics)

**Color Palette:**
- MYCURE Clinical (#0066CC, #003366, #00A86B)
- High contrast for WCAG AAA (7:1 body text)

**Layout:**
```
Hero Section:
├─ Headline: "Transfer stock in 3 minutes, not 15"
├─ Subheadline: "MYCURE saves Philippine clinics 75% of transfer time"
├─ CTA: "Book Free Demo" button (Clinical Blue #0066CC)
└─ Hero image: MYCURE transfer interface screenshot

Features Section:
├─ Feature 1: Real-time visibility
├─ Feature 2: 3-minute transfers
├─ Feature 3: Audit trail
└─ Feature 4: Offline support

Social Proof:
├─ "Clinic A reduced transfer time from 18 to 4 minutes"
└─ Testimonial with photo

Demo Video:
└─ Embedded YouTube video (1:30)

CTA Section:
├─ "Ready to streamline your operations?"
└─ Demo booking form or Calendly embed
```

**WCAG Compliance:**
- Run `/audit-wcag` on landing page
- Color contrast 7:1 (body text)
- Keyboard navigation functional
- Screen reader compatible

**Output:** WCAG-compliant landing page at mycure.md/features/stock-transfer

## Best Practices

**DO:**
- ✅ Maintain consistent messaging across all channels
- ✅ Use MYCURE Clinical palette (#0066CC) for brand recognition
- ✅ Include #MYCURE #CMS in every post (required hashtags)
- ✅ Add captions to videos (accessibility + 80% watch without sound)
- ✅ Test landing page on mobile (80%+ PH users on mobile)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Launch on different days without coordination
- ❌ Use generic Inter/Roboto fonts (violates brand standards)
- ❌ Skip WCAG validation for landing page
- ❌ Forget captions on video (excludes deaf/hard-of-hearing)
- ❌ Post without tracking links (can't measure ROI)

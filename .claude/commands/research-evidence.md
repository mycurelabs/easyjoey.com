Please use the research-synthesis-guidelines skill to document research findings.

**Research Topic:** [What are we researching?]

**Confidence Grading Requirements:**
- **HIGH:** 3+ sources, primary research, recent (2yrs), PH-specific, consistent
- **MEDIUM:** 1-2 sources, some gaps in quality/recency
- **LOW:** Single source, outdated, assumptions, contradictory

**Required Sections:**
1. **Finding Title** [CONFIDENCE LEVEL]
2. **Summary** - 1-2 sentence finding statement
3. **Evidence** - List all sources with dates and specific data
4. **Triangulation Assessment** - How sources validate each other
5. **Limitations** - Gaps, biases, caveats
6. **Implications** - What this means for MYCURE product decisions

**Philippine Healthcare Context:**
- Validate findings are PH-specific (not US/EU extrapolated)
- Consider LGU, RHU, BHS deployment environments
- Account for connectivity, literacy, resource variations

**Recommendations:**
- HIGH confidence → Proceed with decision
- MEDIUM confidence → Validate further if critical
- LOW confidence → Do NOT base major decisions, research more
